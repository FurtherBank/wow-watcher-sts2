The Fool raises the baton. The Watcher takes the stage.

Adds one rare Skill to the Fool's card pool, Wah Watcher!, with the native Wah melody tag.

[b]Beta branch only[/b]
Requires Slay the Spire 2's public-beta branch. Tested with game version v0.113.0.

[b]Required Watcher implementation[/b]
This mod currently depends on [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747526116]Watcher by Boninall, Workshop ID 3747526116[/url].
This is the Boninall Watcher build. The lamali Watcher build is maintained separately. Choose the build matching your Watcher implementation; the two dependencies share an internal mod ID, so enable only the matching dependency.

Also requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3737335127]BaseLib, Workshop ID 3737335127[/url].
Tested dependency versions are Watcher 0.10.10 and BaseLib 3.4.8.

[b]The card[/b]
Wah Watcher!
Rare · 0 Energy · Skill · Wah · Exhaust

Add a random card for entering Wrath, a random card for entering Calm, Rushdown, and Blasphemy to your hand.
When upgraded, all added cards are Upgraded.

Four cards in total. The Wrath and Calm pools exclude the Watcher's starter cards and stance cards with Exhaust. Conditional and delayed stance entries remain eligible.
Added cards keep their own Energy costs. Cards that exceed the hand limit go to the discard pile.

[b]Getting started[/b]
Switch to public-beta, subscribe to this mod and both dependencies linked above, enable them in game, then restart.
The card is added to the Fool's normal card pool and can be obtained through normal gameplay.

[b]Languages and testing[/b]
Supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Italian, Polish, and Russian.
Translations follow the required Watcher mod's card and stance terminology.
Tested on macOS with beta v0.113.0, including normal and upgraded card plays, hand overflow, localized card displays, and portrait lifecycle regression. Windows and Linux have not been tested on actual devices.
